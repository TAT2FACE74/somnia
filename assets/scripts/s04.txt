Pause. Whatever was pulling you forward can wait for one minute.

Feel your feet. Unclench your hands. Soften the space between your eyebrows. Take one slow breath in… and release it fully. Again — in… and out.

Ask yourself quietly: what can wait, and what is the next useful step? Not the whole problem — only the next small, honest action.

Return to the body. Shoulders drop. Jaw soft. Breath steady. The momentum of the difficult moment loosens its grip.

You are reset. Choose that next useful step, and begin again from here — clearer, calmer, ready enough.
