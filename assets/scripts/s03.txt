Create a little space around this crowded moment. Place a hand on your chest or belly if that helps. Feel the rise… and the fall.

Breathe in for a gentle count of four… and out for six. Again. Longer exhales tell the body it can soften.

Name what is here without fighting it: pressure, worry, urgency. Then imagine that weather sitting beside you, not inside you.

Look around the room. Notice three steady things you can see. Feel the ground under you. You are here, now, and this moment can hold you.

With each breath, the storm has more sky around it. You do not have to force calm. You only make room for it.
