Welcome this morning before the noise arrives. Sit or stand tall. Soften the eyes. Feel the first clear breath of the day fill your chest.

Inhale calmly… exhale completely. Let yesterday stay where it belongs.

Ask yourself: what is the one direction that matters most today? Not a list — one honest intention. Hold it quietly in the mind.

Feel your feet on the ground. Feel readiness without rush. Carry this single clear purpose into your first meaningful task.

When distraction comes, return to the breath, then to that one direction. You begin focused. You begin enough.
