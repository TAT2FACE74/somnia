Stop waiting to feel perfectly ready. Readiness often arrives after the first movement, not before.

Stand or sit with energy in the spine. Take a bright breath in… and a decisive breath out.

Connect with one clear reason — why this matters — and one simple movement that begins it. A step. A reach. An open page.

Feel motivation as warmth in the body, not pressure in the mind. You do not need the whole path. You need a useful beginning.

On the next breath, begin. Get up and go — one honest action, now.
