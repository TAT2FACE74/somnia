Stop negotiating with the night. Dim the lights of the mind. You do not need another plan, another review, another problem solved before sleep.

Breathe in softly… breathe out longer. Feel the body grow heavy from the feet upward — calves, thighs, hips, back, shoulders, face.

If a thought tries to reopen the day, whisper inwardly: not now. Tomorrow can hold it. Tonight is for release.

Imagine placing every unfinished thing on a shelf beside the bed. They will wait. You are free to drift.

Eyes closed. Breath slow. Fall into sleep without effort. Rest is already meeting you.
