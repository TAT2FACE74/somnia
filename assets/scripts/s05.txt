Close the day by noticing what held you. Sit quietly. Let the breath become warm and unhurried.

Bring to mind one small, specific moment from today — a kindness, a bite of food, a patch of light, a completed task. See it clearly.

Feel a soft glow of appreciation in the chest. It does not need to be grand. Gratitude lives in the ordinary.

Name one more. And one more. Let those moments settle like warm embers.

Carry that glow into rest. The day is complete. You are enough for tonight.
